
*What is it*

Designed to protect HTTPS applications fronted by External HTTPS load balancers

Enforces security policies to safeguard applications and services from web-based threats

Protection against DDOS, enables IP filtering, geo-based restrictions

*gcloud commands (part of CE)*

*Cloud Firewall vs Cloud Armor*

Firewall operates at the network and infrastructure level

Armor works at the applications level, mainly for HTTPS traffic
