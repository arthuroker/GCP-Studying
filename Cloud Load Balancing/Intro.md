
*What it is*:

Full managed service that distributes traffic across multiple instances

3 benefits:

1) High availability
2) Scalability
3) Optimized performance

Integrates easily with other services like Cloud Armor

It distributes traffic based on routing rules and health checks

*Handling IPs*

Cloud Load Balancing offers static IPs for front-end configurations, ensuring a consistent IP or clients accessing your application
