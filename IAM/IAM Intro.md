IAM allows you mange *who* has what *access* to *which* resources in GCP

**Principal**: any member or identity that can be granted access to GCP resources
	Basically the unit that IAM policies are applied to, perviously known as memebrs

**User Accounts**: associated with a human and needs a username and password to authenticate, used for accessing cloud resources
