
*Motivation*

Answers the problem of how to group a stream of data 

*Three types of windows in Dataflow*

1) Tumbling/fixed
2) Hopping/sliding
3) Session-based

*Tumbling/Fixed windows*

Data is divided into distinct, fixed-sized, non-overlapping intervals of time

Sequential

Usually for over time data

*Hopping/Sliding windows*

Fixed duration, overlap, "hopping" to a new window location

*Session-based*

Defined by a gap of natural inactivity in the data
