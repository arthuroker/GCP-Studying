
*CPU*

General purpose for sequential processing

Small number of powerful cores optimized for handling diverse computational tasks and complex branching logic

Good for pre-training, and training classical ML models

*GPU*

Thousands of smaller, simpler cores optimized for parallel processing of similar tasks simultaneously

Good for deep neural networks

*TPU*

Custom designed by Google for ML workloads

Specialized matrix multiplication units and optimized memory architecture for tensor operations

Good for very large scale DNN training, especially with Tensorflow

In practice, GPUs are sufficient

*Accelerators*

Can control the CPU/GPU ratio by adding GPU accelerators to general purpose or other machine types

*AI Hypercomputer*

Not a service or settings

Just refers to Google's low-latency TPU pods
